# Code of Conduct

SaviaTech is used to support people in vulnerable situations. Contributors are expected to act with
care, respect and discretion.

## Our commitment

We welcome participation regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but listen when someone says they were harmed.
- Protect privacy: never share real personal data, even in examples or screenshots.
- Respect the professional judgement of social workers and the lived experience of the people the
  tool serves.
- Accept responsibility, apologise and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing real case data, credentials or identifying screenshots.
- Ignoring safety, privacy or consent requirements in the interest of speed.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
